# Capital-closure sensitivity, GNI and the period-0 demographics test

Diagnostic reruns for issues #16 (closure / GNI), #15 (2026 resource-constraint residual) and #18 (environment); tracker #27 step 5. Generated by `analysis/closure/summarise.py` from the solve outputs in `analysis/closure/output/`; the prose comes from `analysis/closure/notes.md`. Solves present: 5 of 10.

## Design

* Arms: the no-AI `baseline` and the two ramp arms, `anthropic_ramp` (share_shift_tfp: labour share −3.9pp, +3.1% TFP at fixed inputs) and `obr_ramp` (share_shift: labour share −3.9pp, GDP level unchanged at fixed inputs). Internal ids are `main`'s; the rename in #22 is not merged.
* `zeta_K` ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.5}. 0.1 is OG-UK's default and the committed run; it is re-solved here because the committed results do not export `B`, `K_d`, `K_f`, `D_d` or `D_f`, so there was no GNI for the headline. That makes 9 closure solves, not 6, plus one pre-TP demographics baseline.
* Everything else is held at the committed settings: `zeta_D = 0.4`, `world_int_rate_annual = 0.02`, `epsilon = 1`, `tG1 = 10`, `g_y_annual = 0.010`, common `alpha_G` from each closure's baseline, ramp 2026→2030. **`zeta_K = 0` closes the economy to foreign *private* capital only**: foreigners still absorb 40% of new government debt (`zeta_D`), so `D_f > 0` and GNI < GDP even there.
* Each closure has its own baseline; its ramp arms inherit that baseline's initial assets and debt. Z is re-solved per closure against that baseline's 2026 K and L, exactly as `solve.run_scenarios` does, so every closure carries the same technology target. (Holding Z at the 0.1 values would add a spurious fixed-input TFP difference, because the γ-only gain (K/L)^Δγ − 1 depends on baseline K/L.)
* GNI = Y − r_p·K_f − r_p·D_f. `r_p·K_f` is the return to foreign owners of UK capital, `r_p·D_f` (OG-Core `debt_service_f`) the interest on foreign-held gilts. `r_p` is the rate OG-Core itself charges on K_f in the resource constraint (`get_capital_outflows` is called with `r_p`). δ·K_f is not netted (gross national income). The variant with the firm rate `r` on K_f is in `summary.json` (`gni_gap_r_variant`). Gaps are ratios between arms in the same year, so the common detrending cancels.

## Period-0 demographics test (#15)

OG-UK's `_build_specs` passes `omega_S_preTP` from the UK fetch but never `g_n_preTP`, `rho_preTP` or `imm_rates_preTP`, so period 0 runs on OG-Core's shipped values. The pre-period → 2026 population law of motion (`omega_0[s+1](1+g_n_preTP) = (1−rho_pre[s])·omega_pre[s] + imm_pre[s+1]·omega_pre[s+1]`), relative residual by age:

| pre-TP inputs | min | max | max abs |
|---|---|---|---|
| stock (OG-Core defaults, what runs use) | -5.929% | +2.373% | 5.93% |
| UK-consistent (same fetch) | -0.000% | +0.000% | 2.48e-14% |

`g_n_preTP`: stock 0.007479, UK 0.007410; max |Δ rho_preTP| 1.07e-01; max |Δ imm_rates_preTP| 9.94e-02.
